Where We Work & Locations Served

Clearly displaying our studio availability and mobile detailing coverage zones across the region.

Coverage Area

Our state-of-the-art detailing studio is located in the heart of Detroit, but our mobile service units bring professional care directly to your driveway within a 30-mile radius.

Suburbs & Areas Serviced Daily:

  • Detroit (Studio & Mobile)
  • Grosse Pointe
  • Birmingham
  • Bloomfield Hills
  • Royal Oak
  • Troy
  • Rochester Hills
  • Dearborn

Mobile Detailing Requirements:

  • Power & Water: Rigs are fully equipped with quiet generators & deionized water tanks.
  • Space Clearance: Requires a flat driveway with 5+ feet of clearance around the vehicle.
  • Weather Protection: Heavy rain or freezing temperatures may require indoor space or scheduling.
Outside the 30-mile radius? Contact our studio to discuss custom service options for your vehicle.

Services & Accessibility

To ensure perfect quality, certain high-end treatments require the controlled environment of our physical studio, while other services can be delivered at your convenience.

Ceramic Coating Protection Studio Only

Requires a dust-free environment and precise, climate-controlled curing times for a perfect 9H paint bond.

Paint Correction & Polishing Studio Only

Multi-stage paint leveling requires specialized high-intensity studio lights to identify and eliminate swirls.

Premium Detailing Pack Studio & Mobile

Get the ultimate restoration at our high-tech bay or at your home/office driveway using our fully equipped mobile rigs.

Leather & Upholstery Deep Clean Studio & Mobile

Stain extraction and steam sanitization utilizing our mobile generator and water hookups on-site.
