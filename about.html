Our Origin

Born From a Passion for Perfection

GlossForge was founded in Detroit with one obsession: to deliver detailing perfection. Over 15 years, we have grown from a local garage to a premier state-of-the-art facility by holding every vehicle to strict, ISO-aligned quality standards and certified procedures.

IDA-Certified Specialists

Every detailing technician on our team holds certifications from the International Detailing Association, assuring expert handling.

Ultra-Premium Chemical Standards

We exclusively apply certified ceramic coatings, pH-neutral soaps, and safe non-abrasive clay systems to protect clear coats.

Strict Quality Checklist Review

No car is handed back without passing a comprehensive quality review under high-intensity color-matching lights.

Our Standards

Detailing Without Compromise

How we maintain the highest quality standards in the industry, vehicle after vehicle.

Precision

Paint Depth Measurement

We utilize electronic paint depth gauges before polishing to map clear coat thickness. This ensures we level imperfections safely without risking clear coat burn-through.

Cleanroom

Controlled Coating Rooms

Our ceramic coatings are applied in specialized dust-free, climate-controlled cleanrooms. Controlled temperature and humidity ensure optimal bonding and curing times.

Preservation

pH-Balanced Preservation

We select pH-balanced, silicone-free conditioners and upholstery cleaners that restore the natural suppleness of leather and fabrics without leaving artificial greasy residues.
